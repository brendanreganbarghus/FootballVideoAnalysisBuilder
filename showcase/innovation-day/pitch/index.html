What makes it different — validation, not vibes

Point at pipeline strip on board

"Plenty of demos can draw boxes on players. The hard part is trust. My engine is grounded in the IFAB Laws of the Game and explicit analytics contracts, and every statistic it publishes has passed a guarded human review workflow: a professional records the golden events independently from the video, the engine's events are compared one-to-one, and a segment is only published when they match exactly — fingerprinted and regression-protected."

"So this isn't 'the model said so.' It's evidence in, validated events out — and that's the property that makes it sellable to clubs and federations."
