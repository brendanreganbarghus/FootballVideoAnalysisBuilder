Agreed counting rules

A completed pass is recorded when a player plays the ball and the first following player touch is by the same team. This includes long balls. If possession is lost afterward, the pass remains completed and the later loss is recorded separately as a turnover. Click the pass button at the receiving teammate's first touch, when the pass becomes completed.

A throw-in, goal kick, or other restart also counts as a completed pass when the first receiving player is on the same colour team as the player taking the restart.

Foul and free-kick rule: when a player is fouled, play stops without a turnover. Ignore loose-ball movement after the foul. When the awarded team places the ball and takes the free kick, begin tracking again; record one completed pass only when a teammate first controls the kick. For example, if black controls the ball, white fouls black, and black restarts to a black teammate, record one black completed pass and no turnover.

For this match, “Red/white” identifies the light team and “Black” identifies the dark team. A turnover is assigned to the team that loses possession. Record a shot when an intentional attempt is made toward goal. Use “shot on target” instead when it enters the goal or would enter without a goalkeeper/defender save; it counts as both one shot and one shot on target.

Isolation rule: manual clicks never alter AI events, confidence values, thresholds, or possession inference. The AI panel only replays the saved calibrated baseline.
