What the benchmark currently says

Across three independent one-minute windows, fixed thresholds produce 22/31 correct pass predictions against 46 labels. Shot results are 1/4 correct predictions against 3 labels.

This is a precision-first technical POC. It is not a production accuracy claim. Recall is limited by tiny-ball visibility; shot outcomes need behind-goal cameras and more labeled examples.

Demo runbook

  1. Show the original panoramic camera view and explain fixed-camera input.
  2. Play the annotated video to show player teams and ball trajectory.
  3. Walk left-to-right through the six pipeline stages.
  4. Show provisional totals changing after each overlapping chunk.
  5. Finish with measured results, limitations, and the four-camera roadmap.
