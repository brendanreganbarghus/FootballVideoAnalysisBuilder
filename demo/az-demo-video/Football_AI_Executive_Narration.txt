FOOTBALL INTELLIGENCE PLATFORM - EXECUTIVE PITCH

1. WHY THIS STARTED

This idea started when I studied the needs of an amateur football club. The
club could record a match, but information that might influence football
decisions was often unavailable during the game, delivered too late, or
difficult to trust without checking the video again. I wanted to start at
amateur level because clubs with the least money should still have access to
the standard of evidence normally associated with top professional football.
Coaches need more than footage. They need evidence they can use.

2. THE MARKET AND THE DECISION GAP

Most clubs currently choose between two broad approaches. Accessible camera
platforms record, upload, stream and review matches, commonly through recurring
cloud plans. Professional providers combine tracking, event data, analyst
workflows and integrations, usually through bespoke contracts. Both approaches
add value, but capture, storage, analysis, export and manual verification can
remain fragmented. Costs grow as services are added, cloud connectivity and
provider access may become dependencies, and insight can arrive after the
decision. Completed passes, possession, turnovers and shots matter most while
the match can still be changed.

3. ONE CONNECTED FLOW

The opportunity is not simply another camera or another dashboard. It is one
traceable flow from the match to useful evidence: capture the whole pitch,
understand the state of play, calculate agreed football events, and deliver the
resulting statistics while they still matter. Every published number must
remain connected to the video evidence and the exact rules-engine version that
produced it.

4. THE PROPOSED ARCHITECTURE

Here is the proposed architecture. One elevated panoramic main camera,
positioned on the long side near halfway, continuously preserves the full-pitch
geometry, team spacing and off-ball movement. The blue and red player shapes
show how the calibrated view supports tactical context. Green arrows connect
completed passing lanes. The dashed amber lane shows where possession is lost.
A second opposing panoramic camera is recommended, while two goal-end cameras
remain optional when budget permits.

5. HOW THE PROCESS OPERATES

Operation begins with a rights-cleared panoramic feed. Camera calibration maps
image positions to the pitch. The detector and tracker derive players and the
ball from the raw frames; no provider event file tells the engine what
happened. The law-grounded engine then carries possession and match state
forward and publishes the agreed statistics. Today's proof runs this process
on prepared footage. The target deployment continuously ingests the live feed
and delivers rolling one-minute updates on a locked local GPU. Every
sixty-second window must publish before the next is due, with duplicates
suppressed and unresolved state carried forward.

6. DECISION-READY OUTPUTS

The platform can be applied now to controlled post-match analysis,
analyst-assisted segment review, and an amateur or semi-professional pilot
using prepared footage. The match-day experience is the next delivery step.
Its screens show statistics, not automatic tactical instructions. During play,
the coach sees current totals, possession and zone indicators. At half-time,
that evidence expands into completed passes, turnovers, shots, final-third
entries and recent trends. The coaching team combines the evidence with what
it observed and decides the response.

7. HOW THE RULES ENGINE IMPROVES

Training and validation remain separate from match inference. Detection models
are developed offline using appropriately licensed development data. Held-out
annotations are revealed only after predictions are frozen; they never tell
the engine what event to create. In the Review application, authorised
validators inspect controlled video segments. Copilot contributes an
independent video-based proposal, the rules engine contributes its own result,
and provider annotations can provide a third post-inference check. A supported
disagreement may improve only a general rule. Protected regressions then verify
previous behaviour before the validated engine is deployed to new footage.

8. AFFORDABLE CONFIGURATIONS

For an amateur club, the target installed core is below twelve thousand euros
before tax: one panoramic camera, local GPU, storage, networking, installation,
calibration and initial support. It is a planning target, not a quotation. The
core requires neither cloud processing nor cloud video storage. Camera
subscriptions recur, while bespoke professional video, data, cloud and analyst
packages can collectively reach thousands of euros per month. The fair
comparison is therefore the complete three-year cost of ownership. A second
panoramic camera and goal-end views are optional upgrades, not costs every
amateur club must carry.

9. FROM CURRENT PROOF TO PROFESSIONAL SCALE

Today, the working proof is offline: calibrated footage, tracking, football
state, event review and regression-protected output. Professional scale
requires synchronized camera feeds, sustained GPU throughput, resilient
networking and storage, security, monitoring, systems integration and
match-day support. Live ingest, stadium deployment and durable player identity
remain next-stage capabilities. Future Query By Probability keeps the main
camera continuous and consults only a relevant secondary view during a short
low-confidence moment. The controlled pilot target is at least ninety-five
percent against independently reviewed references, reported separately for
each agreed statistic.

10. THE INVITATION

We propose a staged co-development pilot between a professional football club
and Xebia. Together we can define the statistics and targets, validate
controlled matches, and build a route from grassroots affordability to
professional performance. Xebia can help productionise edge processing,
security, resilient live ingest, systems integration, monitoring and
operational scale. The goal is not more data after the moment has passed. It
is trusted football intelligence while the next decision can still change the
match. Thank you for taking the time to review this vision. I hope we can build
it together.
