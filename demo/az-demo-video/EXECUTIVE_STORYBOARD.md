## Claim boundaries

- **Current:** offline prepared-video proof, calibration, tracking, football
  state, focused event review, fingerprints, and protected regressions.
- **Current but provisional:** pass, turnover, possession, and developing shot
  outputs.
- **Future:** live ingest, production multi-camera operation, stadium and
  dugout delivery, Query By Probability, and durable player identity.
- **95%:** proposed controlled-pilot target measured separately by statistic
  against independently reviewed references.
- **One-minute updates:** target deployment requirement. Every 60-second window
  must be processed and published before the next window is due; the current
  offline proof does not establish that production latency yet.
- **Below EUR 12,000 before tax:** target for the installed amateur-club core,
  including one panoramic camera, local GPU, storage/networking, installation,
  calibration, and initial support. It is a planning target, not a quotation.
- **Market comparison:** compare complete three-year ownership cost. Recurring
  entry-level subscriptions and bespoke professional video, data, cloud, and
  analyst services are different categories; do not claim one universal
  competitor price.
- The closing proposes co-development; it does not imply an existing club
  endorsement, contract, or deployment.
- The sales-facing build requires an explicitly owned or commercially licensed
  match clip through `FOOTBALL_EXECUTIVE_MATCH_VIDEO`. The non-commercial
  Simula Alfheim research footage and derived screenshots are excluded.
