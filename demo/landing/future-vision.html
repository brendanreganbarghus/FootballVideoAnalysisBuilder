Multi-camera coverage for larger stadiums — “Query by Probability”

Goal-end camera A
↔
Main touchline camera
↔
Goal-end camera B
↑ query on low confidence ↑
Opposite touchline camera

The manifest format already supports multiple synchronized camera streams with independent time offsets and calibration; cross-camera fusion is the next planned pipeline stage. The idea: one main panoramic camera per side plus goal-end cameras, where the primary camera queries the neighbouring feeds only when its own detection confidence is low, to corroborate a decision rather than replace the main view. For larger matches this runs on a local, on-premises server — not a cloud dependency — keeping club video under club control.

This prototype was developed by a focused team to prove the concept quickly and honestly. If AZ Alkmaar wants to take the platform further, Xebia Netherlands can bring additional AI and engineering capacity — across AI, Microsoft/Azure, Copilot, agentic AI and enterprise architecture — so the team can scale to match the ambition and timeline of the next phase.
