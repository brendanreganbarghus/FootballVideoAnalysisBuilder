Continuous, incremental processing

Configurable 30s or 60s segment chunks
→
Law-grounded match-state engine
→
Statistics screen, appended as processed

The same controlled-segment discipline shown earlier can eventually run continuously and configurably — rolling 30-second or 60-second chunks processed one after another through the existing law-grounded match-state engine and its analytics event state machines — appending newly validated statistics to the screen as more of a match is processed, instead of a human hand-picking every window. In this future vision the rules engine itself would also be refreshed on a regular, e.g. daily, cadence as more segments are independently reviewed — always through the same general, evidence-based, regression-protected process, never a per-segment shortcut.
