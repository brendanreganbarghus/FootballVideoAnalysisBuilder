Query By Probability

A forward-looking multi-camera concept: one primary camera carries the whole stream by default. Extra cameras are consulted only for the specific moments where the primary view's own confidence drops — never continuously, and never for the full match.

What it is: when ball or player detection confidence on the Main Camera drops for particular frames or a short segment, the system queries only the relevant secondary camera(s) whose field of view actually covers that moment — a goal-end camera for goal-mouth ambiguity, a touchline camera for a wide-play ambiguity — and fuses that targeted evidence back in to strengthen the decision.

What it is not: it does not process or query every secondary stream for the whole match. The Main Camera remains the default single source of evidence; other cameras are consulted on demand, for the ambiguous moment only, then the system reverts to the Main Camera.

Future concept

Not implemented, not partially implemented, and not assumed anywhere in the current pipeline — a roadmap idea for larger, multi-camera stadium deployments.

Targeted, not exhaustive

Confidence-triggered queries only reach the camera(s) relevant to the ambiguous moment, on a local/on-premises deployment where required — not a standing multi-stream analysis job.
